## Community Guidelines — Echo

**Last updated:** 23 March 2026  
**Effective date:** 23 March 2026

These guidelines set Echo’s **platform-wide** expectations. **Communities (servers) may adopt their own stricter rules** for members and moderation, but they cannot override platform-wide safety requirements.

---

## 1) Expression & Conduct

Echo protects free expression without regard for popularity, disagreement, or offense. No one is removed simply for what they say or believe. Limits apply only when the effects of that expression threaten the platform’s existence, disrupt its operation, or materially prevent others from using it.

Though individual communities on Echo may enforce stricter standards within their own spaces, including limiting or removing content or members that do not align with their norms.

---

## 2) NSFW Content

NSFW content is restricted and only allowed under strict conditions:

NSFW content is only permitted in:
servers explicitly marked as `18+`, or
private chats where all participants are confirmed to be `18+`, or
groups where all members are `18+`

Any NSFW content outside of these environments is not allowed.

---

## 3) Violence & Harm

The following is strictly prohibited:

Graphic depictions of real-world violence
Gore or explicit violent imagery
Content that glorifies real harm

Discussion of real-world events is allowed only when non-graphic and informational in nature.

---

## 4) Threats & Safety Risks

Credible threats of violence are strictly forbidden.
Any behavior interpreted as a real-world threat may result in immediate enforcement action.

---

## 5) Spam & Platform Abuse

The following behaviors are not allowed:

Spam (repeated, irrelevant, or disruptive messaging)
Mass messaging or disruptive automation behavior
Server raiding or coordinated disruption attempts

---

## 6) Self-Promotion

Self-promotion, advertising, or unsolicited marketing is not allowed on the platform.

---

## 7) Automation & Bots

Botting or automated behavior that simulates user activity or disrupts the platform is not allowed unless explicitly authorized by the platform.

---

## 8) Account Usage

Alt accounts or multiple accounts per user are not allowed.

---

## 9) Identity & Impersonation

Impersonation of other users, individuals, or entities is strictly prohibited.

Users are allowed to remain anonymous and are not required to use real-world identity.

---

## 10) Personal Information & Safety

Doxxing (sharing private or sensitive personal information about others) is strictly prohibited.

---

## 11) Content Ownership

Users retain ownership of the content they create.

Rights and usage of content are defined in the Terms of Service, including any limited platform usage rights granted for operation, moderation, or service functionality.

---

## 12) Enforcement Philosophy

Echo enforces these guidelines through a combination of automated systems and manual review.

The platform reserves the right to take action where:

user behavior threatens platform stability
legal obligations require intervention
abuse or exploitation of the system occurs

---

## 13) Third-party notices

For credits and license notices relating to open-source software, emoji assets, and UI icons used in the Service, see **Attributions** under Settings → **Legal** → **Terms & policies**.

---

## 14) Contact

For safety concerns, moderation questions, legal questions, or other support needs, use in-app reporting tools where available or contact **[support@app-echo.net](mailto:support@app-echo.net)**.
