## Privacy Policy — Echo App

**Last updated:** 23 March 2026  
**Effective date:** 23 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what data Echo (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, why we collect it, what is optional, and what choices you have when using the Echo mobile and web app (the “Service”).

We operate as a registered business in Germany (Kleingewerbe) and comply with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), where applicable.

---

## 1) Information We Collect

We collect only the data needed to run and secure the Service. Some data is required to create and use an account, and some is optional.

### 1.1 Account Information

- Email address (**required** for account creation)
- Phone number (**optional**, only if you add it)
- Username and account identifiers
- Login and verification data (**required** for the sign-in method you choose)

### 1.2 Identity & Access Data

- IP address
- Device information (for example device model, OS, browser)
- Device identifiers or similar technical identifiers (used for security and anti-abuse)

### 1.3 User Content

- Messages, posts, and other content you choose to submit through the Service
- Media and files you choose to share through the Service

### 1.4 Metadata & Usage Data

- Activity logs (for example timestamps, message delivery status, usage events)
- Interaction data (for example engagement signals, moderation signals, feature usage)

### 1.5 Analytics & Safety Data

- Aggregated or de-identified analytics to improve performance and reliability
- Safety and abuse-prevention signals used for moderation and enforcement (for example spam, illegal content, and ban-evasion detection)

We process user-generated content and related metadata only to provide the Service, protect users, and enforce our rules.

---

## 2) How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

### 2.1 Service Operation

- Provide messaging and communication functionality
- Deliver, store, and manage content so it is available as intended
- Maintain account functionality (including authentication, session management, and basic security)

### 2.2 Security, Moderation, and Enforcement

- Detect spam, abuse, fraud, and illegal content
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Prevent system abuse and ban evasion (including technical measures such as IP/device-based restrictions and account linking when needed for enforcement)
- Protect the Service and users from misuse, security threats, and policy violations

### 2.3 Service Improvement

- Improve features and system performance
- Debug and correct errors
- Improve usability and reliability

### 2.4 Automated Processing

We use automated systems to:

- Classify content
- Detect abuse and policy violations
- Rank, filter, or limit content when required by safety rules

Where required by law, automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are reviewed by a human on request or as required by regulation. Automated risk signals do not automatically guarantee a final enforcement decision in every case.

### 2.5 Legal Compliance

- Comply with legal obligations
- Respond to lawful requests from authorities
- Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

---

## 3) Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under GDPR, we process personal data based on one or more of the following legal bases:

- **Contract performance:** to provide the Service and fulfill obligations arising from your use of the Service.
- **Legitimate interests:** for security, abuse prevention, moderation, analytics, and improving reliability and user safety.
- **Legal obligations:** to comply with laws and regulatory requirements.
- **Consent:** where specifically required by applicable law (for example, for certain processing involving minors where required).

---

## 4) Data Sharing

We share personal data only in the situations listed below:

### 4.1 Service Providers (Sub-processors)

- Hosting and infrastructure providers
- Backend and messaging providers that operate core Service functions
- Security and moderation service providers that help enforce safety rules
- Analytics and monitoring providers used to keep the Service stable and performant

### 4.2 Legal and Safety Disclosures

- Law enforcement or regulators, when disclosure is legally required
- Other parties only when disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, or security and legally permitted

We do **not** sell your personal data.

We do **not** share personal data for advertising resale or data-broker purposes.

---

## 5) International Data Transfers

We primarily store and process data within the European Economic Area (EEA), including Germany/EU-based infrastructure.

If an international transfer is necessary (for example, because a sub-processor operates outside the EEA), we use GDPR-compliant safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or another legally valid transfer mechanism.

---

## 6) Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to provide the Service, keep it secure, enforce rules, and meet legal obligations.

### 6.1 Active Data

- Data needed for account operation is kept while your account is active.

### 6.2 Deleted Accounts / Deleted Content

- After you delete your account or content, limited residual data can remain temporarily in backups or logs.
- Residual data is kept only for backup integrity, fraud prevention, and legal compliance, and removed according to our retention schedule.

### 6.3 Retention Periods

Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirement. We do not keep data indefinitely by default.

---

## 7) Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights under GDPR:

- Right of access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability (where applicable)
- Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Right to withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent)

You can request to exercise these rights through in-app support or by emailing **[support@app-echo.net](mailto:support@app-echo.net)**.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

---

## 8) Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
- Encryption at rest for supported storage systems
- Access controls that limit internal access to authorized personnel
- Monitoring and abuse-detection systems

No system is 100% secure, but we apply security controls that are appropriate for the Service and update them over time.

---

## 9) Automated Decision-Making

We use automated systems for moderation and safety.

These systems:

- help detect abuse and spam
- may limit or restrict content visibility
- may trigger enforcement actions (such as removing content, warning, limiting access, or suspending/terminating accounts)

Where required by law, decisions with significant effects are eligible for human review.

---

## 10) Children’s Data

The Service is available to minors only where legally permitted.

We process children’s personal data only in line with applicable law and required safeguards. If required consent or eligibility cannot be verified, we restrict features or suspend the account until verification is completed.

---
